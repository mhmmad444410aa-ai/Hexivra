# Hexivra privacy notice

This notice covers the offline build of Hexivra 2.0.0 maintained in this repository. The game stores puzzle progress, settings, and rewards on the device and uses the device date to select the daily puzzle. This build includes no advertising SDK, analytics, account sign-in, or upload of gameplay data. Android may manage application data under the backup settings of the device.

To remove local data, clear Hexivra's storage in Android Settings. Settings can open external reference links in your browser. The destination's privacy policy applies to your use of that website.

For privacy questions, use the [Hexivra project issue tracker](https://github.com/mhmmad444410aa-ai/Hexivra/issues). Keep personal data out of public issues.

## Português (Brasil)

Este aviso vale para a versão offline 2.0.0 de Hexivra mantida neste repositório. O jogo salva progresso, configurações e recompensas no dispositivo e usa a data local para escolher o desafio diário.
